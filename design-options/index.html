Fortinet channel partner platform · design exploration

Three ways to build the partner dashboard

Same data, same seller, three different bets on what makes a cybersecurity sales crowd open a dashboard twice a day. All three are FortiSOC-literate — dark fabric, flowing telemetry, severity language — but each commits to a different personality. Every chart passes the same palette validator, ships hover + keyboard tooltips, a data-table twin, and full prefers-reduced-motion support.

OPTION 01

Fabric Command

The FortiSOC homage. The partner's business rendered as a living fabric — product pillars stream into an attainment core that feeds the four numbers a seller runs the quarter on. Ambient, cinematic, demo-room gravity.

OPTION 02

The Desk

A trading terminal for security deals. Ticker tape of promos, a live deal book with tick-by-tick flashes, expiry clocks on every SPIFF. Treats quota like a market position — pure adrenaline for a commission-driven crowd.

OPTION 03

The Briefing

An executive morning memo, not a monitoring wall. Editorial type, calm scroll-triggered motion, a ranked attention list, and best-next-moves. The face you'd happily project in a QBR — and the sharpest contrast to FortiSOC.
